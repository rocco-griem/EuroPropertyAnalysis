## Candidate sources (to be evaluated)
- **National house price index** — Eurostat `prc_hpi_a` (EU; 2015=100); UK **not** in Eurostat,
  use ONS HPI for London/UK. BIS Residential Property Price database / OECD as cross-checks.
- **CPI / inflation** — Eurostat HICP (annual average index); ONS CPI for the UK.
- **Income / wage** — Eurostat earnings/wage indices or OECD average annual wages (country-level).
- **City-level property index** — fragmented; sourced per city from national statistics offices
  where a credible series exists.
